# Code of Conduct

KeyBridge welcomes respectful participation from users, testers, and contributors. These expectations apply to repository issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions, and other project spaces.

## Our standards

- Be considerate and constructive. Critique ideas and code, not people.
- Assume good faith, especially when someone is reporting a difficult-to-reproduce bug.
- Respect different backgrounds, skill levels, and opinions.
- Do not harass, insult, threaten, discriminate against, or deliberately intimidate anyone.
- Do not publish another person's private information, connection codes, pairing tokens, or screenshots without their permission.
- Do not use this project or its community to coordinate unauthorized access to computers.

## Reporting and enforcement

For a non-sensitive concern, contact the maintainers through a repository issue. For a sensitive conduct concern, contact the repository owner privately through the contact information on their GitHub profile; if that is not possible, use GitHub's content-reporting tools. Do not disclose another person's private information in a public report. Security vulnerabilities should instead follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

Maintainers may edit or remove harmful content, request a change in behavior, temporarily restrict participation, or ban repeat or serious offenders. Reports will be reviewed with discretion, and retaliation against a reporter is not acceptable.
